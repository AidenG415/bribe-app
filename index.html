Common questions

How do I change which apps are limited? Open Bribe → Limits → Limited apps.

Why can't I raise my limit right away? Lowering takes effect immediately; raising waits 24 hours so you can't talk yourself out of it in the moment.

How do refunds work? Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
