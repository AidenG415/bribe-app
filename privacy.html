Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Bribe does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers.

What stays on your iPhone

Your daily limit, settings, the apps you choose to limit, usage checkpoints, and your purchase history are stored only on your device. Apple's Screen Time keeps your app choices private; Bribe never sees what you do inside those apps.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Bribe receives only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, never your payment details.

Sharing

If you share your weekly card, the image is created on your device and sent only where you choose to share it.

Children

Bribe does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue.
